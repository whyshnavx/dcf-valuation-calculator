DCF Methodology

The model forecasts five years of Free Cash Flow and discounts those cash flows using the Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC).

Terminal Value is calculated using the Gordon Growth Method. Enterprise Value is then adjusted for cash and debt to determine Equity Value.

FCF = EBIT × (1 − Tax Rate) − CapEx − Change in NWC

Terminal Value = FCF₍ₙ₊₁₎ ÷ (WACC − Terminal Growth)
