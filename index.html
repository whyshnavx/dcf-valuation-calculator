08 · Methodology

How the model works

The core financial logic behind the valuation.

08

Discounted Cash Flow

Forecast five years of operating performance, calculate free cash flow and discount each period using WACC.

FCF = NOPAT + D&A − CapEx − ΔNWC

Trading Comparables

Peer EV/Revenue and EV/EBITDA multiples are applied to the selected company's operating metrics to estimate a market-based equity value.

Precedent Transactions

Acquisition multiples are used to estimate the value a strategic or financial buyer might pay for control.
